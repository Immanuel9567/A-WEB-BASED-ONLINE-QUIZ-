Everything an assessment workflow needs

A complete prototype covering question banking, secure timed delivery, automated grading and result analytics.

⚡

Instant auto-grading

Single-choice, multiple-response and true/false questions are graded server-side the instant an attempt is submitted — no manual marking.

📡

Real-time monitoring

Teachers watch a live session feed: who is writing, who has submitted, current scores and time remaining — refreshed every couple of seconds.

⏱️

Server-enforced timers

The countdown is authoritative on the server. When time expires, the system auto-submits and grades whatever the student has answered.

📊

Analytics & item analysis

Score distributions, pass rates and per-question facility indices help teachers spot questions that are too easy, too hard or misleading.

🔐

Roles & secure access

Teacher and student accounts with hashed passwords and bearer-token sessions. Students never receive correct answers before grading.

🛡️

Integrity signals

Per-attempt question shuffling, attempt limits, tab-switch logging and auto-saved answers protect the assessment's credibility.

How it works

Three steps from question bank to graded result sheet.

1

Teacher builds the quiz

Create a quiz, set duration, pass mark and attempt limits, then add questions to the question bank.

2

Students take it live

Timed delivery with auto-saved answers, a question palette and a server-side countdown that auto-submits on expiry.

3

Results process in real time

Scores, pass/fail verdicts, answer reviews, leaderboards and analytics update the moment attempts land.
